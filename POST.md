# Claude Usage Tracker

**Ever hit your Claude session limit mid-flow with no warning? I built a tiny
tray app so you never do.**

It sits in your system tray / menu bar and shows your Claude subscription usage
as a live percentage bar — the same "Session (5-hour)" number `/usage` and
claude.ai show, always one glance away. Click it for a full Account & Usage
panel: your session, weekly, and per-model limits with reset countdowns.

- 🟢 At-a-glance bar that shifts green → amber → red as you approach your limit
- 🖥️ **Windows, macOS and Linux** from one codebase
- 🔐 Reads the token Claude Code already stores locally — no extra login, and
  your token only ever talks to Anthropic's own servers
- 🎨 Yknot-branded, links straight to our team dashboard

Free and open source 👉 https://github.com/EPOHnomeL/Usage-Windows-Tracker

Built at [Yknot](https://y-knot.io).
